Trading Rules
Follow these rules in order. Analyze the market and its moves, show your work, and explain the thought process behind every move.
## Entry rules
 Trade the New York session and the Asia session daily.
 Be active and ready 15-30 minutes before market open.
 Mark the highs and lows of the previous session. These are your key levels.
 Wait for a breakout.
 Once the market closes above or below a key level, enter the trade.
 Use a 3:1 risk-to-reward ratio.
 If the market is choppy and not volatile, do not force a trade.
Managing the trade
 Move the stop loss to breakeven once the trade is $25 in profit.
 Take profit at $50. Only do this manually if 15+ minutes have passed and the take profit hasn't triggered automatically.
 Explain and journal
 Before each trade, explain why you are about to take it.
 After each trade, explain how it went and what could have gone better.
Keep a trade journal: entry time, exit time, profit or loss, what you traded, and why.
