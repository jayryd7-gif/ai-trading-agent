# Details: government, adoption, and what is coming

## 5. US government and regulation

I found no new federal crypto action dated Saturday or Sunday. These items are from the past week.

**Congress**
- **CLARITY Act (H.R. 3633, Digital Asset Market Clarity Act).** It would set up a federal market-structure framework for crypto.
  - The House passed it 294-134 in July 2025. The Senate Banking Committee voted 15-9 in May 2026 (DataWallet).
  - The Senate cloture vote failed 49-50 on Sept 15, so it is stalled (CNBC, American Banker). The Senate is not due back until Nov 9 (Baker Botts).
  - Sticking points were official conflicts of interest, rules for non-custodial developers, and stablecoin yield (Baker Botts).
- **H.R. 10357, Digital Asset Tax Certainty Act (low-profile).** It would amend the tax code. It would exempt small transactions from reporting and clarify how mining and staking rewards are taxed. Ways and Means reportedly amended it and sent it to the House floor 38-5 on Sept 16. It has not passed the House (Bitcoin Foundation site, single source).
- **Fairshake.** A Fairshake affiliate disclosed about $7M more in ads against Sen. Sherrod Brown in Ohio, bringing its total past $46M (Decrypt, FEC filing).

**Agencies**
- **CFTC.** On Oct 5 it opened an advance notice of proposed rulemaking on retail crypto trading. It floats "Regulation CTX" for leveraged retail trades and "Regulation CAM" for a new type of crypto exchange registration (Baker Botts, Troutman).
- **SEC.** On Oct 14, Jamie Selway, SEC Director of Trading and Markets, said five CFTC-registered exchanges have filed to register as security futures exchanges. He expects at least four to start perpetual futures on securities this quarter (Troutman).
  - The SEC also proposed a custody framework for advisers and funds that allows self-custody and state trust company custodians (Oct 9, Orrick).
  - It has proposed "Regulation Crypto Assets" and issued temporary relief for tokenized securities venues (Baker Botts).
- **FinCEN (quiet rule change).** On Oct 9 it withdrew its proposed rule on crypto mixing and its proposed unhosted-wallet reporting rule (Orrick).
- **Treasury.** A GENIUS Act interim final rule was published Sept 30 (Federal Register 2026-19966). It reportedly sets a $10B threshold for stablecoin issuers (Yahoo Finance).
- **White House.** On Oct 14, Patrick Witt, executive director of the President's Council of Advisers on Digital Assets, said the priority is securing and accounting for the government's existing bitcoin. He said no purchase strategy is set (TradingView/Crypto Briefing).
